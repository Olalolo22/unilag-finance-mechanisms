# FBA 411: Business Policy & Strategic Management

**Department:** Management Sciences / Finance | **Units:** 2 | **Exam Mode:** CBT (Computer-Based Test)

---

## 1. Course Overview & Core Topics
FBA 411 covers strategic formulation, execution, and organizational longevity:
* **Strategic Analysis Frameworks:** SWOT, PESTEL, Porter’s Five Forces, Industry Value Chains.
* **Competitive Advantages & Moats:** Cost leadership, differentiation, focus strategies, resource-based view (VRIO framework: Valuable, Rare, Inimitable, Organized).
* **Corporate Diversification & Integration:** Horizontal vs vertical integration, strategic alliances, joint ventures, M&A strategies.
* **Strategy Implementation & Governance:** Agency problems, stakeholder alignment, balanced scorecards, organizational change management.

---

## 2. The On-Chain Mechanism Analogue

### A. Porter's Five Forces & Protocol Defensibility (Vampire Attacks)
* **TradFi:** Threat of new entrants and substitute products erosive to industry profit margins.
* **Protocol Analogue:** Defending AMMs and lending protocols against open-source forks and vampire attacks (e.g. SushiSwap vs Uniswap, LooksRare vs OpenSea). Why liquidity alone is not a moat; switching costs, network effects, and integrations create durable defensibility.

### B. Agency Theory & Token Governance Alignment
* **TradFi:** The principal-agent conflict between shareholders (principals) and managers (agents). Solved via stock options, board oversight, and corporate governance.
* **Protocol Analogue:** Token governance failure modes (e.g. low turnout, governance attacks, voter apathy, treasury draining). Designing lockups (veTokenomics like Curve `veCRV`), optimistic governance, and mandate boundaries (*CRESCO* style) to align token holders with long-term protocol survival.
